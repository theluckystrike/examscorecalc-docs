SAT and ACT
===========

The digital SAT reports 400-1600 as the sum of two 200-800 section scores; the ACT reports
four 1-36 section scores and a composite average. Neither is a linear function of raw
points, so both require the concordance and conversion tables published by the test makers.

When checking an estimate, compare the section score rather than the composite: section
errors cancel in an average and hide the mismatch.

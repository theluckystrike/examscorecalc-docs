ExamScoreCalc methodology
=========================

`ExamScoreCalc <https://examscorecalc.com/>`_ converts raw practice-test points into an
estimated AP (1-5), SAT (400-1600) or ACT (1-36) score. The estimate is arithmetic over
published conversion data, not a prediction of the real exam.

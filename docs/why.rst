Why a raw score is not a test score
===================================

Every one of these exams is equated: two students with the same raw points on different
test dates can receive different scaled scores. The scaled number compensates for the
difficulty of that specific form.

So a raw-to-scaled converter can only ever produce an estimate from the most recently
published conversion tables. Treat it as a smoke test - "am I near a 4 or near a 2?" -
not as a score report.
